# QTalk 1.1.1-beta(3) — LAN2-AUDIO3

- Ordinary and conference call microphone icons are smaller (13×20 dp); the 42 dp buttons and compact widget stay the same size. Muted icons use the same dimensions.
- Ringtone selection now has visible Play/Stop controls. Play previews the whole file at the selected ringtone volume. EOF, Stop, another selection, Save, Cancel, leaving settings, calls and engine shutdown release playback. Delayed loading cannot restart a stopped preview. Imports and their 60-second limit remain available.
- Unknown unsaved numbers have Clear on the left and Add contact on the right in Information. Clear empties the complete dial draft. It is disabled for an empty draft and throughout incoming, dialing, ringing, active and ending calls. Saved-contact Delete/Edit and contact-edit drafts keep their existing behavior.
- Normal microphone and speaker volume are attenuation only: 100% is unity (0 dB), 50% is half amplitude (about −6 dB), and 0% is silent. Changes apply smoothly after releasing the slider and save after a short debounce. Manual mute, speaker mute and hold remain independent. LAN2 staged legs stay muted while gain changes.
- A separate microphone boost switch adds at most +6 dB and defaults OFF. No meter, automatic microphone capture, Windows hardware-volume change, AGC change or EQ change is added.

On the first launch of this build, audio gain settings migrate once to schema 3. Old 50% and 100% become new 100%; old boosts are clamped to unity without enabling boost. Old 0% meant −30 dB, so it becomes approximately 3.16% rather than silently changing to mute. Missing values start at unity. Subsequent launches retain the new values; reset restores unity and boost OFF idempotently.

The display version is `1.1.1-beta(3)`; About and the JAR identify `LAN2-AUDIO3`. LAN capability/protocol stays `LAN2`, with the existing handoff diagnostics and conference behavior retained.

Verification uses Compose semantics/rendered bounds, controllable fake playback, PCM samples and synthetic native calls with file audio. Physical microphone/speaker quality, acoustic preview volume, actual installation and multi-PC LAN behavior remain user tests. The installed QTalk and live settings/credentials were not used for validation.
